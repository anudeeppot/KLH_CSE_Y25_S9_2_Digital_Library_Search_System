Title: Fast String Searching with Boyer Moore Algorithm
Author: Robert S. Boyer, J. Strother Moore
Category: String Algorithms
Year: 1977
ISBN: 978-0131103856
Type: Research Paper
Keywords: Boyer-Moore, bad character rule, good suffix rule, pattern shifting, sublinear search
Abstract: The Boyer-Moore algorithm scans characters from right to left, utilizing the bad-character and good-suffix heuristics to skip large sections of text, frequently achieving sublinear search speeds in natural language.
Citations: 01_kmp_algorithm.txt, 04_suffix_array_indexing.txt
Content: The Boyer Moore algorithm is considered the benchmark for string searching in natural language corpora. It matches characters from right to left within the pattern window. When a mismatch occurs, it applies two powerful heuristics: the Bad Character Heuristic and the Good Suffix Heuristic. These rules allow the pattern to jump forward by multiple positions rather than advancing by one character, often yielding sublinear average performance O(n / m).
