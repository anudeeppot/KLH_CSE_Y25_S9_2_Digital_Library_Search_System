Title: Vector Space Model and TF-IDF for Information Retrieval
Author: Gerard Salton, Christopher D. Manning
Category: Information Retrieval
Year: 1975

Type: Journal
Keywords: TF-IDF, cosine similarity, vector space model, document similarity, text mining
Abstract: The Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) weighting scheme transforms unstructured document texts into vector representations to evaluate thematic similarity via cosine angle.
Citations: 05_edit_distance_dp.txt, 11_natural_language_processing.txt, 16_web_search_pagerank.txt
Content: The vector space model represents documents as multidimensional term vectors. Term Frequency (TF) measures how often a word occurs within a document, while Inverse Document Frequency (IDF) discounts ubiquitous words across the entire corpus. By calculating the Cosine Similarity between two document vectors, the digital library determines topical relatedness and recommends closely linked research papers, books, and journals.
