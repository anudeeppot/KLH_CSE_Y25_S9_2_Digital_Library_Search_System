Content: Speech and Language Processing provides a comprehensive mathematical and algorithmic foundation for natural language processing and computational linguistics. It starts from fundamental string operations such as tokenization, minimum edit distance, and regular expressions, advancing through n-gram language models, hidden Markov models, vector semantics, transformers, and large language models for information extraction in modern digital libraries.
