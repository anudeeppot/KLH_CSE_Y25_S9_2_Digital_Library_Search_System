Title: Oxford Academic: Global Sequence Alignment for Manuscript Revision
Author: Saul B. Needleman, Christian D. Wunsch
Category: Sequence Alignment & Bio-Informatics
Year: 2021
ISBN: 978-0198854203
Type: Research Paper
Keywords: Needleman-Wunsch, sequence alignment, dynamic programming, manuscript diff, gap penalty
Abstract: Global sequence alignment using dynamic programming models the progression of historical manuscript editions, academic errata, and version diffs with affine gap penalties.
Citations: 05_edit_distance_dp.txt, 11_natural_language_processing.txt
Content: The Needleman-Wunsch algorithm performs optimal global alignment between two textual sequences in O(N * M) time. In digital library archive management, comparing successive revisions of research manuscripts or legal charters identifies insertions, deletions, and lexical substitutions through backtracked DP matrices, preserving scholarly editorial provenance.
