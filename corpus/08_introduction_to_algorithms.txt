Content: Introduction to Algorithms, commonly known as CLRS, provides a rigorous and comprehensive survey of modern computer algorithms and data structures. It details algorithmic design paradigms such as divide-and-conquer, greedy techniques, and dynamic programming. The text thoroughly analyzes string matching algorithms including KMP, Rabin-Karp, and finite automata, alongside essential graph algorithms like Breadth-First Search, Depth-First Search, Dijkstra, and Bellman-Ford.
