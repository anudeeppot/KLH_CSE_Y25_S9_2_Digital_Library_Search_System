Title: IEEE Xplore: Network Flow and CDN Throughput Optimization
Author: Andrew V. Goldberg, Robert E. Tarjan
Category: Network Flow & Optimization
Year: 2022
ISBN: 978-0769562814
Type: Research Paper
Keywords: Edmonds-Karp, Dinic, Max-Flow, Min-Cut, CDN, throughput, bottleneck
Abstract: Network flow algorithms model digital repository content distribution across academic CDN nodes. Edmonds-Karp and Dinic level-graph algorithms compute maximum delivery capacity while Min-Cut identifies saturated links.
Citations: 07_citation_graph_analysis.txt, 19_spectrum_scalable_computing.txt
Content: Digital library systems serve thousands of concurrent academic downloads across tiered content delivery networks. By mapping source repositories, regional proxy caches, and campus gateway nodes into a directed capacity network, maximum document throughput is modeled as the maximum s-t flow. Dinic algorithm constructs layered DAGs via BFS and drives blocking flows in O(V^2 E) time, whereas the Min-Cut theorem isolates the exact saturated link bottlenecks.
