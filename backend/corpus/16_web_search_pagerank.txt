Title: The Anatomy of a Large-Scale Hypertextual Web Search Engine
Author: Sergey Brin, Lawrence Page
Category: Information Retrieval
Year: 1998
ISBN: 978-0131105337
Type: Research Paper
Keywords: PageRank, web search, hypertext, inverted index, crawling, graph centrality
Abstract: The original Google architecture paper presenting PageRank, an algorithm leveraging hyperlink graph topology to compute objective relevance rankings for document retrieval.
Citations: 06_tf_idf_similarity.txt, 07_citation_graph_analysis.txt, 08_introduction_to_algorithms.txt
Content: This historic research paper presents the design of the original Google search engine. The authors introduce PageRank, an algorithm that interprets the directed citation graph formed by hyperlinks as an endorsement system, calculating stationary probability distributions of random surfers. The paper also details crawler architectures, forward indexes, and inverted index data structures optimized for sub-second retrieval across millions of hypertext documents.
