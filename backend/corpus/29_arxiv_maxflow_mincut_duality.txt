Title: arXiv: Max-Flow Min-Cut Duality in Multi-Tenant License Allocation
Author: Jon Kleinberg, Eva Tardos
Category: Network Flow & Optimization
Year: 2024
ISBN: 978-0321263162
Type: Research Paper
Keywords: Max-Flow Min-Cut, bipartite matching, license allocation, Edmonds-Karp, Dinic
Abstract: Formulates the restricted digital library multi-tenant book and journal license allocation problem as maximum flow in a bipartite capacity network with min-cut duality verification.
Citations: 24_ieee_network_flow_cdn.txt, 07_citation_graph_analysis.txt
Content: When university students and researchers request simultaneous access to constrained electronic book licenses, naive allocation leads to starvation. Transforming user borrow requests and journal license limits into a directed flow network permits polynomial-time optimal matching via Edmonds-Karp or Dinic. By the Max-Flow Min-Cut Theorem, the minimum capacity cut directly isolates license shortages and student contention sets.
