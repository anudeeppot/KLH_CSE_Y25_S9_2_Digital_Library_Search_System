Title: Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare
Author: Eric Topol, Fei-Fei Li, Andrew Ng
Category: Artificial Intelligence
Year: 2022
ISBN: 978-0465097500
Type: Research Paper
Keywords: artificial intelligence, healthcare, clinical decision support, medical imaging, deep learning, diagnostics, clinical trials
Abstract: A comprehensive survey of artificial intelligence in healthcare, focusing on clinical decision support systems, computer vision in radiology, and ethical considerations.
Citations: 11_natural_language_processing.txt, 12_deep_learning_goodfellow.txt, 21_machine_learning_healthcare.txt
Content: Artificial intelligence in healthcare is revolutionizing clinical practice and hospital workflows. Clinical decision support systems powered by deep learning and medical computer vision assist physicians in detecting anomalies from imaging modalities such as MRI and CT scans. The paper analyzes algorithmic robustness, data privacy in clinical trials, and multi-modal neural network architectures for health informatics.
